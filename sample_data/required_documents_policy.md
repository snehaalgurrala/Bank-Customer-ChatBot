# Required Documents Policy

The minimum document set for a personal loan includes identity proof, income proof, and recent bank statements.

## Identity proof

Acceptable identity proof includes a government-issued photo ID. The name and date of birth should match the customer profile.

## Income proof

Income proof can include recent salary slips, tax returns, employer letters, or verified business income records.

## Bank statement

Recent bank statements help verify salary credits, recurring obligations, bounced payments, and average account balance.

## Additional documents

The bank may request address proof, existing loan statements, co-applicant documents, or collateral details depending on the product and risk profile.
