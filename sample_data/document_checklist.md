# Loan Document Checklist

## Identity
- Government-issued photo ID
- Social Security number or tax identification number where applicable
- Date of birth and contact details

## Income
- Recent pay stubs or salary slips
- Bank statements
- Tax returns or Form W-2 / 1099 equivalents
- Employer or business details

## Loan-specific
- Property documents for mortgage loans
- Vehicle quotation for auto loans
- Existing debt statements for debt consolidation

## Upload quality
Customers should upload clear, complete, readable files. Blurry scans, cropped forms, and password-protected PDFs can delay review.
