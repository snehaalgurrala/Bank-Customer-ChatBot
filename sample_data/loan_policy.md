# Sample Loan Policy

## Eligibility
Applicants should have verifiable identity, stable income, and an active bank account. Employment can be salaried, self-employed, or contract-based if income can be documented.

## Affordability
Monthly repayment capacity is reviewed using income, existing EMI obligations, requested loan amount, tenure, and credit history. A high existing EMI burden can increase risk and may require a lower loan amount or additional review.

## Decision categories
- `approved`: Application meets baseline policy and document checks.
- `manual_review_required`: Application needs admin review due to risk, missing details, or borderline affordability.
- `needs_documents`: Customer must provide clearer or additional documentation.
- `rejected`: Application does not meet policy requirements at this time.

## Risk review
Risk score is an internal indicator. It should not be shown as a guaranteed approval or rejection. Staff should use it with document verification and responsible lending checks.
