# Loan Product FAQ

## Personal loans
Personal loans can be used for debt consolidation, emergency expenses, education, or planned purchases. Applicants typically need stable income, acceptable credit history, and proof of identity.

## Home loans
Home loan applications usually require income documents, property details, identity proof, address proof, bank statements, and an appraisal or valuation report.

## Auto loans
Auto loans commonly require vehicle quotation details, down payment information, identity proof, income proof, and insurance information before disbursement.

## Credit score guidance
A higher credit score can improve approval likelihood and may help the customer qualify for better rates. Customers with lower scores may still qualify with additional documentation, collateral, or a co-applicant.

## Responsible lending reminder
The chatbot should explain loan concepts, required documents, and next steps. It should not promise approval, quote guaranteed rates, or replace a licensed loan officer.
