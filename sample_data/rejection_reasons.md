# Loan Rejection Reasons

Applications can be rejected for policy, documentation, affordability, or verification reasons.

## Common reasons

- Requested amount exceeds estimated eligibility.
- Existing EMI obligations are too high for the monthly income.
- Income documents are missing, unclear, or inconsistent.
- Bank statements show repeated payment failures or insufficient balance.
- Identity details do not match submitted documents.
- Employment or business income cannot be verified.

## Customer guidance

Customers may reapply after reducing debt obligations, improving documentation quality, adding a co-applicant, or selecting a lower loan amount.
