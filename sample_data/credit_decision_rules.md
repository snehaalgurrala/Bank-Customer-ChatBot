# Credit Decision Rules

Credit decisions combine application data, document verification, affordability, and internal policy checks.

## Approved

An application may be approved when income is verified, documents are clear, EMI capacity is sufficient, and risk score is low or acceptable.

## Manual review required

Manual review is required when documents are incomplete, income is variable, EMI burden is high, or risk score is medium to high.

## Needs documents

The decision should remain needs documents when required files are missing, unreadable, expired, or inconsistent with the application.

## Rejected

An application may be rejected when affordability is insufficient, documents cannot be verified, identity checks fail, or credit policy requirements are not met.
