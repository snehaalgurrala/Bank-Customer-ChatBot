# Personal Loan Eligibility Policy

Personal loan applicants should have stable monthly income, valid identity proof, and a repayment profile that leaves enough disposable income after existing EMIs.

## Income assessment

Disposable income is calculated as monthly income minus existing EMI. A customer should generally have enough disposable income to support the expected EMI and routine living expenses.

## EMI capacity

Estimated EMI capacity is capped at 40 percent of monthly income. Existing EMI obligations reduce this capacity. Applications that exceed EMI capacity should be reviewed for a lower amount, longer tenure, or co-applicant.

## Employment type

Salaried customers usually have lower income volatility. Self-employed and contract customers may require stronger bank statements, tax returns, or business income proof.
